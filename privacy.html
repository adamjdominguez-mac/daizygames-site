Angel Dash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Angel Dash is a mobile game made by Adam Dominguez ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. In short: the game has no accounts, keeps your progress on your own device, and the only information that leaves your device goes to our advertising partners (only if you watch an ad) and to Apple or Google when you buy something.

Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress is saved only on your device: levels reached, coins, unlocked weapons and upgrades, best scores, settings such as sound and controls, and whether you bought REMOVE ADS. We do not receive this information, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information, and the game has no sign-in.

Advertising

Angel Dash shows only optional "rewarded" ads: you choose to watch one in return for something in the game, such as a continue or bonus coins. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource) and Unity Ads, both run by Unity Technologies.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these services may collect information from your device such as:

  • device and app details (device model, operating system and version, language, app version);
  • your IP address and the approximate location that can be worked out from it (country or region);
  • on Android, your device's advertising ID; on iPhone and iPad, an identifier for this app's developer (IDFV);
  • how you interact with ads (whether an ad was shown, watched to the end or tapped).

The game never asks iPhone users for permission to track them across other companies' apps and websites, so the iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or to our ad partners. On every device, the game tells our ad partners that you have not consented to personalised advertising and that your information may not be sold. The ads you see are therefore not based on your activity in other apps.

Unity's handling of this information is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

If you buy REMOVE ADS, the game no longer starts the advertising services at all, from the next time you open it.

Purchases

The REMOVE ADS purchase is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game only whether REMOVE ADS has been bought, so it can be restored on a new device. Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to the purchase itself.

Children

Angel Dash is made for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • You never have to watch an ad. Every ad in the game is optional.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved on your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests about advertising data are best sent to Unity using the link above; we are happy to help if you contact us.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the date at the top.
